AUTOMATED WORKFLOWS

Nobody ever says no. They just stop replying.

A full cart and no order. A lead who asked one question in March and went quiet. An appointment on Saturday that nobody has confirmed. Your agent notices all three, waits a sensible amount of time, and picks the conversation back up.

What it does

It watches for the thing that did not happen.

Most automation waits for someone to click something. This waits for someone to not click something, which is the more useful signal and the harder one to catch.

It knows how long to leave it.

Thirty minutes for a cart, because any longer and they have moved on. The day before for an appointment. A month for a customer who has gone quiet. You set it once and stop thinking about it.

It does not just send and hope.

The customer replies asking whether it will arrive before Friday. Your agent answers that, then reopens the checkout in the same breath. An email cannot do the second half. ---

The four workflows

Recover carts Someone leaves with a full cart. Half an hour later, if the order still has not gone through, your agent messages them on WhatsApp, deals with whatever was stopping them, and puts them back on the checkout page. Follow up leads An enquiry comes in. Your agent works out whether it is worth your sales team's afternoon, files it in your CRM, sends a short note, and offers times that are genuinely open in your calendar. Remind customers A meeting is on Saturday. On Friday your agent sends the reminder and lets them confirm or shift it without anyone making a phone call. Re-engage A customer has not bought anything in a month. Your agent sends something connected to what they bought last time, handles whatever comes back, and tells you who returned. ---

Where it works

Workflows run wherever the customer already is. Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, or your standalone page. ---

What it connects to

Shopify · WooCommerce · HubSpot · Zoho CRM · Google Calendar · Calendly ---

FAQ

Do I have to build these myself?

No. All four arrive ready to switch on. Change the wait time and the wording if you want, or leave them alone.

What if the customer writes back?

Your agent handles it. That is the whole difference between this and a scheduled email.

Can I stop one halfway through?

Yes. Switch a workflow off and anyone currently inside it stops hearing from you immediately. --- ---
